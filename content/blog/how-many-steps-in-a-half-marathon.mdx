# How Many Steps in a Half Marathon?

**How many steps in a half marathon?** Running a half marathon takes roughly **16,000–19,000 steps**, while walking the same 13.1-mile distance comes out to about **25,000–30,000 steps**. The gap comes down to stride length — runners cover far more ground per step, so they need thousands fewer steps to finish.

A half marathon is **13.1 miles (21.1 kilometers)** — exactly half the distance of a full marathon. Whether you are training for your first half, walking it for charity, or just curious how the numbers add up, this guide breaks down exactly how many steps a half marathon takes by height, pace, and activity type.

## Quick Answer: Steps in a Half Marathon

Here is the short version for an average adult (5'7" / 170 cm):

- **Walking a half marathon:** ~27,000 steps
- **Brisk walking a half marathon:** ~26,000 steps
- **Running a half marathon:** ~17,500 steps

These averages assume a walking stride of about 0.78 meters and a running stride of 1.1–1.3 meters over the full 21,100-meter distance. Your personal step count depends on your height, leg length, pace, and terrain.

## Walking a Half Marathon vs Running: Key Differences

How many steps a half marathon takes depends heavily on whether you walk or run. Here is a side-by-side comparison:

| Factor | Walking a Half | Running a Half |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| Average steps | 25,000–30,000 | 16,000–19,000 |
| Average time | 3.5–5 hours | 1.5–2.5 hours |
| Stride length | 0.64–0.88 m | 0.91–1.37 m |
| Calories burned (160 lb) | 1,000–1,300 cal | 1,400–1,700 cal |
| Impact on joints | Low | Moderate to high |
| Best for beginners | Yes | Build up gradually |

Both walking and running a half marathon are major endurance achievements. Walking is gentler on the joints and accessible to almost anyone with enough training, but it takes far longer and racks up thousands more steps. Running burns more calories per minute and finishes sooner, but demands more conditioning. Many first-timers use a walk-run approach, alternating between the two to manage fatigue.

## How a Half Marathon Compares to Your Daily Step Goal

A half marathon is not just a workout — it is several days' worth of steps packed into one session:

- **Walking a half marathon (~27,000 steps):** That is about **2.7 days** of a 10,000-step goal in a single outing.
- **Running a half marathon (~17,500 steps):** That is roughly **1.75 days** of a 10,000-step goal.

To put it another way, if your target is 10,000 steps a day, walking a half marathon covers your movement for almost three full days at once. Even running it puts you nearly two days ahead. That is why race day completely transforms your weekly step average — many trackers will show a single spike taller than the rest of the week combined.

## Why Stride Length Matters So Much Over 13.1 Miles

Stride length is the biggest variable in your half-marathon step count, and over 21,100 meters even small differences add up to thousands of steps.

**Walking stride length** averages about 0.78 meters for an adult of average height. At that stride, a half marathon takes roughly 27,000 steps. But a shorter walker with a 0.64 m stride needs nearly 33,000 steps — almost 6,000 more for the exact same course.

**Running stride length** averages 1.1–1.3 meters, which brings the half-marathon count down to about 16,000–19,000 steps. Elite runners with strides over 1.5 meters can finish in under 14,500 steps.

Factors that influence your stride length include:

- **Height and leg length** — the single most important factor
- **Pace** — faster walking or running lengthens your stride
- **Terrain** — hills and trails shorten each step
- **Fatigue** — stride naturally shortens late in a long race
- **Footwear** — supportive shoes help maintain a natural stride

You can estimate your own stride length by walking a known distance (say 20 meters), counting your steps, and dividing the distance by the number of steps.

## How Long Does It Take to Walk or Run a Half Marathon?

Finishing times for 13.1 miles span a wide range depending on fitness and pace:

- **Walking (moderate, 3.0 mph):** ~4 hours 22 minutes
- **Brisk walking (3.5–4.0 mph):** ~3 hours 15–45 minutes
- **Beginner runner (6.0 mph):** ~2 hours 11 minutes
- **Intermediate runner (7.0 mph):** ~1 hour 52 minutes
- **Advanced runner (8.5 mph):** ~1 hour 33 minutes
- **Elite runner (13+ mph):** under 1 hour

Most recreational half-marathon runners finish between **1 hour 45 minutes and 2 hours 30 minutes**. Walkers should plan for **3.5 to 5 hours**, and many half-marathon events set a generous course cutoff to accommodate them. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many steps is a half marathon?

A half marathon is approximately **16,000–19,000 steps when running** and **25,000–30,000 steps when walking**. The average runner of medium height (5'7") takes about 17,500 steps to finish 13.1 miles, while the average walker takes around 27,000. Your exact count depends mostly on your height and stride length.

### How many steps is a half marathon walking?

Walking a half marathon takes roughly **25,000 to 30,000 steps** for most adults, with shorter walkers reaching 32,000+ and taller walkers closer to 24,000. The average walker logs about 27,000 steps over the full 13.1-mile (21.1 km) distance.

### How many miles is a half marathon?

A half marathon is **13.1 miles**, or **21.1 kilometers** — exactly half the 26.2-mile (42.2 km) distance of a full marathon. The official distance is 21,097.5 meters.

### How long does it take to walk a half marathon?

Most people walk a half marathon in **3.5 to 5 hours** at a pace of 3.0–4.0 mph. A steady 3.5 mph brisk walk finishes in about 3 hours 45 minutes, while a more relaxed 3.0 mph pace takes around 4 hours 22 minutes. Terrain, rest breaks, and fitness all affect your time.

### How many steps is a full marathon?

A full marathon (26.2 miles) is roughly **double** a half marathon: about **32,000–38,000 steps running** and **50,000–55,000 steps walking** for an average-height adult. See the comparison table above for how it lines up against shorter race distances.

### Does walking or running a half marathon burn more calories?

Running a half marathon burns more total calories than walking it — roughly 25–30% more for the same distance — because the higher intensity raises your per-minute burn. That said, walking still burns over 1,000 calories for most people, since you are on your feet far longer.
