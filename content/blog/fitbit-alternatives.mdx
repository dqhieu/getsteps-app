# Best Fitbit Alternatives for iPhone (2026)

<TLDR>
The best **Fitbit alternative** for most iPhone users is **Steps: Workout & Pedometer** — it counts steps using your iPhone's built-in sensors (no wearable to buy), then adds free GPX export, 23+ native workout types, 8 widget types, and a Wrapped-style yearly recap with no ads. Fitbit is really built around its hardware; without a device, the Fitbit app is limited. For cross-platform challenges, Pacer is the closest match. For pure minimalism, try Pedometer++.
</TLDR>

Fitbit is a hardware-first ecosystem: most of its best features — continuous heart rate, sleep stages, automatic activity detection — require you to buy and wear a Fitbit tracker. On iPhone, the Fitbit app without a device falls back to "MobileTrack," which counts steps from your phone but drops most of the good stuff. Fitbit Premium (now rebranding to Google Health Premium) runs $9.99/month on top.

That's exactly why iPhone owners look for **Fitbit alternatives** — apps that give you rich step and workout tracking using the sensors already in your phone and Apple Watch, no extra hardware required.

## Why Look for a Fitbit Alternative?

Fitbit is excellent if you own the wearable. Here's why people on iPhone look elsewhere:

- **You don't want to buy a device** — the Fitbit app alone is limited on iPhone
- **Premium paywall** — coaching, advanced insights, and sleep tools sit behind a $9.99/month subscription
- **No Apple ecosystem fit** — Fitbit doesn't offer real iPhone widgets or a native complications experience
- **You want free GPX export** — to save and share routes without a subscription
- **Uncertain future** — Fitbit is being folded into Google Health, and the brand has been in transition

The good news: switching costs nothing. Apps like Steps read from Apple HealthKit, so any steps already logged on your iPhone follow you automatically.

## Best Fitbit Alternatives for iPhone in 2026

### 1. Steps: Workout & Pedometer (Best Overall)

[Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) is the most complete Fitbit alternative for iPhone users who don't want a wearable. It counts steps through HealthKit and your iPhone's motion chip, then layers on the features Fitbit reserves for its hardware or Premium tier: 8 home/lock/StandBy widget types, a native Apple Watch app with complications, 23+ workout types with GPS, **free GPX export**, daily goal streaks, 8 personal record categories, and a Wrapped-style yearly recap. Core features are free, with an optional Steps Pro upgrade.

**Best for:** iPhone users who want Fitbit-style tracking and motivation without buying a Fitbit or paying for Premium.

**Why it stands out:** Fitbit's best features need the band. Steps gives you glanceable widgets, native multi-sport tracking, and a yearly recap using hardware you already own.

### 2. Pacer (Best for Cross-Platform & Challenges)

Pacer is the closest match to Fitbit's social and challenge features, and like Fitbit it runs on **both iPhone and Android**. It offers step tracking, guided walking programs, and group challenges with friends and family. If you mostly used Fitbit for community and step goals across a mixed-phone household, Pacer fits.

**Best for:** Mixed iPhone/Android groups who love challenges and streaks.

### 3. Pedometer++ (Best Minimalist Option)

Pedometer++ is a long-running, trusted step counter for iPhone and Apple Watch. It focuses on steps, walks, and hikes with a clean, no-clutter interface. If you only ever looked at your Fitbit's step count, Pedometer++ is the calm, focused alternative.

**Best for:** Users who want the simplest possible step counter with a proven track record.

### 4. Apple Health (Best Built-In, No Install)

Apple Health counts your steps automatically in the background — no app or device needed. It's the data source every other iPhone pedometer reads from. The trade-off: no home screen widgets, streaks, or workout-logging tools of its own.

**Best for:** Minimalists fine with opening the Health app manually and installing nothing extra.

## How to Switch from Fitbit to Steps

Switching is fast because your iPhone step data already lives in Apple Health.

### Step 1: Install Steps

[Download Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) and grant HealthKit access. Any steps your iPhone has already logged load instantly — no export from Fitbit needed.

### Step 2: Add a Widget

Long-press your home screen, tap **+**, search for Steps, and choose a widget. The circular progress ring shows steps, goal progress, and calories at a glance — something the Fitbit app never offered on iPhone.

### Step 3: Set Your Step Goal

Open Settings and set your daily target. Not sure what to aim for? Our [daily step goal calculator](/tools/daily-step-goal-calculator) suggests a number based on your age and activity level.

### Step 4: Track a Workout and Export It

Start any of 23+ workout types, then export the route as a GPX file — free, no subscription. It's a core reason people switch from Fitbit's paywalled ecosystem.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best alternative to Fitbit?

For most iPhone users, Steps: Workout & Pedometer is the best Fitbit alternative. It tracks steps and workouts using your iPhone and Apple Watch — no wearable to buy — and adds free GPX export, 8 widgets, personal records, and a yearly recap.

### Can I use the Fitbit app without a Fitbit device?

Yes, but it's limited. On iPhone the Fitbit app offers "MobileTrack," which counts steps from your phone's sensor. Heart rate, sleep stages, and automatic activity tracking all require an actual Fitbit device.

### Are Fitbit alternatives as accurate?

For step counting, yes. Phone-based pedometers on iPhone read from the same motion sensors, so counts are comparable. Fitbit's edge is heart-rate and sleep data from its wearable — not step accuracy.

### Do I lose my data if I stop using Fitbit?

Your iPhone step history is stored in Apple Health, so any HealthKit app like Steps keeps working. Data logged inside the Fitbit cloud can be exported from your Fitbit account before you leave.

### Is there a Fitbit alternative for Android?

Pacer runs on both iPhone and Android, making it a good cross-platform pick. Steps and Pedometer++ are iOS and Apple Watch only.
