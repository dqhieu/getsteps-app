# How Many Steps Is Half a Mile? (Chart by Height)

**How many steps is half a mile? About 1,100 steps for the average adult walking at a normal pace. Most people land between 1,000 and 1,300 steps depending on height and speed — shorter walkers take closer to 1,300, taller walkers closer to 1,000. Half a mile takes roughly 10 minutes to walk.**

Half a mile is one of the most useful distances to know by heart. It's the walk to the far end of a big parking lot, a lap and a bit of most neighborhood blocks, or two laps of a standard track. Here's exactly what it costs you in steps.

## The Short Answer

| Pace | Steps in Half a Mile | Time |
|------|---------------------|------|
| Slow stroll (2 mph) | 1,250–1,300 | 15 min |
| Casual (2.5 mph) | 1,180–1,230 | 12 min |
| Normal (3 mph) | 1,100–1,150 | 10 min |
| Brisk (3.5 mph) | 1,050–1,100 | 8.5 min |
| Power walk (4 mph) | 1,000–1,050 | 7.5 min |
| Jog (5 mph) | 800–900 | 6 min |

Use **1,100 steps** if you want a single number to plan around. It's the midpoint for an average-height adult at an ordinary walking pace.

## The Math Behind It

Half a mile is 2,640 feet, or 31,680 inches. Divide that by your step length and you have your answer:

**31,680 ÷ your step length in inches = steps in half a mile**

An adult's walking step length runs about 41–43% of their height. At 5'6" that's roughly 27.5 inches:

31,680 ÷ 27.5 = **1,152 steps**

The same logic scales to any distance — half a mile is simply half of your steps-per-mile figure. If you already know you take 2,300 steps per mile, half a mile is 1,150. Our [Step Distance Calculator](/tools/step-distance-calculator) handles any distance you throw at it.

## Steps in Half a Mile, by Height

At a normal 3 mph walking pace:

| Height | Step Length | Steps in Half a Mile |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| 5'0" | 24.8" | 1,275 |
| 5'2" | 25.6" | 1,235 |
| 5'4" | 26.4" | 1,200 |
| 5'6" | 27.3" | 1,160 |
| 5'8" | 28.1" | 1,125 |
| 5'10" | 28.9" | 1,095 |
| 6'0" | 29.7" | 1,065 |
| 6'2" | 30.6" | 1,035 |

The gap from 5'0" to 6'2" is about 240 steps — roughly a 20% swing. If you're at either end of that range, the generic "1,000 steps" estimate you'll see quoted elsewhere will be off by more than you'd like.

Women average more steps than men over the same half mile, simply because average height differs. See [how many steps in a mile for a woman](/blog/how-many-steps-in-a-mile-for-a-woman) for a women's chart.

## How Long Does It Take to Walk Half a Mile?

At the average adult walking speed of 3 mph, half a mile takes **10 minutes**.

| Pace | Speed | Time for Half a Mile |
|------|-------|---------------------|
| Slow | 2.0 mph | 15 minutes |
| Casual | 2.5 mph | 12 minutes |
| Average | 3.0 mph | 10 minutes |
| Brisk | 3.5 mph | 8 min 35 sec |
| Fast | 4.0 mph | 7 min 30 sec |
| Jog | 5.0 mph | 6 minutes |

Ten minutes is a genuinely handy benchmark. It's the length of a post-meal walk, a phone-call walk, or the movement break that breaks up a long stretch of sitting. Three half-mile walks in a day gets you to 1.5 miles and roughly 3,400 steps without ever setting aside a workout block.

For longer distances, see [how long does it take to walk 1 mile](/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-walk-1-mile).

## How Many Calories Does Half a Mile Burn?

Walking burns roughly 0.03 calories per pound of body weight per minute at a moderate pace. Over a 10-minute half mile:

| Body Weight | Calories Burned |
|-------------|----------------|
| 120 lbs | 36 |
| 150 lbs | 45 |
| 180 lbs | 54 |
| 200 lbs | 60 |
| 250 lbs | 75 |

A useful rule of thumb: **half a mile burns about 25–30% of your body weight in calories divided by 10** — or more simply, around 45–55 calories for a typical adult. Walking uphill or at a brisk pace pushes that higher.

## What Half a Mile Looks Like in Real Life

Sometimes it's easier to picture the distance than to count it:

- **Two laps** of a standard 400m outdoor track (the inside lane)
- **8–10 city blocks** in a typical US grid (north–south blocks are shorter)
- **About 18 lengths** of an American football field including end zones
- **10 minutes** of ordinary walking
- **Roughly 5 minutes** of easy jogging

If you're building a walking habit from scratch, half a mile is an excellent first target — short enough to finish on a low-motivation day, long enough to count. Our [beginner walking workout plan](/blog/walking-workout-plan-beginners) builds up from exactly this kind of distance.

## Common Questions

### Is half a mile 1,000 steps?

Close, but slightly low for most people. 1,000 steps is half a mile only if your step length is about 31.7 inches — taller than average. Most adults take **1,100 to 1,200 steps** to cover half a mile. Use 1,000 only if you're around 6'2" or walking briskly.

### How many steps is a quarter mile?

About 550 steps for an average adult, or one lap of a standard outdoor track. Just halve your half-mile figure: if half a mile is 1,150 steps for you, a quarter mile is roughly 575.

### Is walking half a mile a day enough exercise?

It's a good start but below the general activity guidelines. Half a mile is about 1,100 steps and 10 minutes — useful if you're currently sedentary, and far better than nothing. Health authorities recommend working toward 150 minutes of moderate activity per week, so aim to build up to 2–3 miles a day over time. See [is walking enough exercise](/blog/is-walking-enough-exercise).

### How many steps is 1.5 miles?

About 3,450 steps for an average adult at a normal pace — triple the half-mile figure. Multiply your personal half-mile number by three for a more accurate estimate.

### Does walking half a mile uphill take more steps?

Yes. Uphill walking shortens your stride, so the same half mile takes roughly 5–15% more steps depending on the grade — around 1,200–1,300 instead of 1,100. It also burns significantly more calories. See [incline walking benefits](/blog/incline-walking-benefits).

## The Bottom Line

Half a mile is about **1,100 steps** and **10 minutes** of walking for the average adult. Shorter walkers should expect closer to 1,250, taller walkers closer to 1,050, and anyone moving briskly will land at the low end of the range.

It's a small enough distance to fit into any day and a big enough one to add up. Four half-mile walks — one at each meal plus one before bed — is 4,400 steps and two miles, built entirely out of ten-minute pieces.
