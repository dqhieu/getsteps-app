# Best StepsApp Alternatives for iPhone (2026)

<TLDR>
The best **StepsApp alternative** for most iPhone users is **Steps: Workout & Pedometer** — it counts steps just like StepsApp, then adds free GPX export, 23+ native workout types, 8 widget types, and a Wrapped-style yearly recap with no ads. For pure minimalism, try Pedometer++. For cross-platform challenges, Pacer is the closest match to StepsApp. Every HealthKit app counts steps identically, so choose on features and price, not accuracy.

> **Quick note on names:** *StepsApp* (one word, by Apptorials) is a different app from *Steps: Workout & Pedometer* — the app this guide recommends. They're easy to mix up, so we'll always write the competitor as "StepsApp."

StepsApp is a polished, popular pedometer with 20M+ users across iPhone and Android. But its free tier shows ads, and features like GPS workout tracking and advanced analytics sit behind StepsApp Pro — which is exactly why many people start searching for **StepsApp alternatives**.

This guide covers the best StepsApp alternatives for iPhone in 2026, what each is good at, and how to switch in seconds (no data migration required).

## Why Look for a StepsApp Alternative?

StepsApp is genuinely good at clean step tracking, streaks, and social challenges. Still, here's why people look elsewhere:

- **Ads in the free tier** — a fully ad-free experience requires StepsApp Pro
- **Paywalled GPS and analytics** — GPS workout tracking and advanced insights are Pro-only
- **You want free GPX export** — to save and share your routes without a subscription
- **You want broad native workouts** — multi-sport tracking for running, cycling, hiking, yoga, strength, and more, built in
- **You want a yearly recap** — a Wrapped-style summary of your year in steps
- **You want a fresh design** — sometimes a change of scenery is reason enough

The best part: switching costs you nothing. Every quality pedometer reads from Apple HealthKit, so your step history follows you automatically.

## Best StepsApp Alternatives for iPhone in 2026

### 1. Steps: Workout & Pedometer (Best Overall)

[Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) is the most complete StepsApp alternative. It counts steps through HealthKit, then layers on the features StepsApp gates or skips: 8 home/lock/StandBy widget types, a native Apple Watch app with complications, 23+ workout types with GPS, **free GPX export**, daily goal streaks, 8 personal record categories, and a Wrapped-style yearly recap. Core features are free, with an optional Steps Pro upgrade.

**Best for:** iPhone users who want a pedometer *and* a full workout tracker without ads or a hard paywall on the basics.

**Why it stands out:** StepsApp keeps GPS workouts and ad-free use behind Pro. Steps gives you native multi-sport tracking, GPX export, and a yearly recap in a generous free tier — making it the natural step up.

### 2. Pedometer++ (Best Minimalist Option)

Pedometer++ is a long-running, fan-favorite step counter for iPhone and Apple Watch. It focuses on steps, walks, and hikes with a clean, no-clutter interface. If StepsApp feels like too much app for you, Pedometer++ is the calm, focused alternative.

**Best for:** Users who want the simplest possible step counter with a trusted track record.

### 3. Pacer (Best for Cross-Platform & Community)

Pacer is the closest match to StepsApp's social side, and like StepsApp it runs on **both iPhone and Android**. It offers step tracking, guided walking programs, GPS routes, and group challenges with friends. If you mainly love StepsApp for challenges but want a different home, Pacer fits.

**Best for:** Mixed iPhone/Android groups and people motivated by community challenges.

### 4. Apple Health (Best Built-In, No Install)

Apple Health counts your steps automatically in the background — no app needed. It's the data source every other pedometer reads from. The trade-off: no home screen widgets, streaks, or workout-logging tools of its own.

**Best for:** Minimalists fine with opening the Health app manually and installing nothing extra.

## How to Switch from StepsApp to Steps

Switching is fast because your data already lives in Apple Health.

### Step 1: Install Steps

[Download Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) and grant HealthKit access. Your full step history loads instantly — no manual export or import from StepsApp needed.

### Step 2: Add a Widget

Long-press your home screen, tap **+**, search for Steps, and choose a widget. The circular progress ring shows steps, goal progress, and calories at a glance — a familiar look if you're coming from StepsApp's ring.

### Step 3: Set Your Step Goal

Open Settings and set your daily target. Not sure what to aim for? Our [daily step goal calculator](/tools/daily-step-goal-calculator) suggests a number based on your age and activity level.

### Step 4: Track a Workout and Export It

Start any of 23+ workout types, then export the route as a GPX file — free, no Pro required. It's a core reason people switch from StepsApp.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best alternative to StepsApp?

For most iPhone users, Steps: Workout & Pedometer is the best StepsApp alternative. It matches StepsApp on step counting and streaks, then adds free GPX export, 23+ native workout types, 8 widgets, personal records, and a yearly recap.

### Is StepsApp free?

StepsApp is free to download but shows ads, and features like GPS workout tracking and advanced analytics require StepsApp Pro. Many people look for alternatives to avoid the ads or subscription.

### Are StepsApp alternatives as accurate?

Yes. Every pedometer that uses Apple HealthKit reads from the same iPhone and Apple Watch motion sensors, so step counts are identical. Accuracy doesn't vary between apps — features and pricing do.

### Will I lose my step history if I switch?

No. Your step history is stored in Apple Health, not locked inside StepsApp. Any HealthKit-based alternative like Steps loads your full history the moment you grant access. No migration needed.

### Is there a StepsApp alternative for Android?

StepsApp itself runs on Android, and so does Pacer if you want a different option. Steps and Pedometer++ are iOS and Apple Watch only.
