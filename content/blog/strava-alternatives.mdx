# Best Strava Alternatives for iPhone (2026)

<TLDR>

Strava is the go-to social network for runners and cyclists — segments, leaderboards, clubs, and kudos. But it isn't really a step counter, its subscription costs $11.99/month, and features like route building from GPX files and full analytics sit behind that paywall. That's why many people search for **Strava alternatives** that focus on everyday step and activity tracking without the social pressure or the price.

This guide covers the best Strava alternatives for iPhone in 2026, what each is good at, and how to switch.

## Why Look for a Strava Alternative?

Strava is excellent for competitive athletes. Still, here's why people look elsewhere:

- **It's not a step tracker** — Strava is built around recorded runs and rides, not daily steps
- **$11.99/month subscription** — segments, route building, and advanced analytics require Strava's paid plan
- **Route creation is paywalled** — exporting your own activities is free, but building routes from a GPX file needs a subscription
- **Social pressure** — not everyone wants a public feed, kudos, and leaderboards
- **You want iPhone widgets and a yearly recap** — glanceable progress without opening the app

The good news: switching costs nothing. Apps like Steps read from Apple HealthKit, so your history follows you automatically.

## Best Strava Alternatives for iPhone in 2026

### 1. Steps: Workout & Pedometer (Best Overall)

[Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) is the best Strava alternative for people who want personal, everyday tracking instead of a social feed. It counts steps through HealthKit and adds 8 home/lock/StandBy widget types, a native Apple Watch app with complications, 23+ workout types with GPS, **free GPX export** (including your routes), daily goal streaks, 8 personal record categories, and a Wrapped-style yearly recap. Core features are free, with an optional Steps Pro upgrade.

**Best for:** iPhone users who want a complete step and workout tracker without a subscription or a public feed.

**Why it stands out:** Strava puts route building and analytics behind $11.99/month. Steps gives you free GPX export, glanceable widgets, and a yearly recap in a generous free tier.

### 2. Pacer (Best for Community & Challenges)

Pacer keeps the motivational, social side of fitness — group challenges and guided walking programs — but centers it on walking and steps rather than competitive segments. It runs on **both iPhone and Android**, making it great for mixed-phone groups.

**Best for:** People who liked Strava's challenges but want a walking-first, cross-platform app.

### 3. Nike Run Club (Best Free Guided Running)

If you mainly used Strava to track and improve your runs, Nike Run Club offers audio-guided runs, training plans, and run streaks — all free. It's running-focused, so it won't track daily steps or multi-sport activity as broadly as Steps.

**Best for:** Runners who want coaching and structure without a subscription.

### 4. Apple Health (Best Built-In, No Install)

Apple Health logs your steps and workouts automatically in the background — no app needed. It's the data source every other iPhone tracker reads from. The trade-off: no widgets, streaks, or social and coaching tools of its own.

**Best for:** Minimalists fine with opening the Health app manually.

## How to Switch from Strava to Steps

Switching is fast because your activity data already lives in Apple Health.

### Step 1: Install Steps

[Download Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) and grant HealthKit access. Your step and workout history loads instantly.

### Step 2: Add a Widget

Long-press your home screen, tap **+**, search for Steps, and choose a widget. The circular progress ring shows steps, goal progress, and calories at a glance — something Strava doesn't offer.

### Step 3: Set Your Step Goal

Open Settings and set your daily target. Not sure what to aim for? Our [daily step goal calculator](/tools/daily-step-goal-calculator) suggests a number based on your age and activity level.

### Step 4: Track a Workout and Export It

Start any of 23+ workout types, then export the route as a GPX file — free, including custom routes, with no subscription. It's a core reason people switch from Strava.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best alternative to Strava?

For most iPhone users who want everyday tracking rather than a social feed, Steps: Workout & Pedometer is the best Strava alternative. It adds free GPX export, 8 widgets, 23+ workout types, personal records, and a yearly recap in a free tier.

### How much does Strava cost?

Strava is free to download, but its subscription is $11.99/month (or $79.99/year). Route building from GPX files, segment leaderboards, and advanced analytics require the paid plan.

### Is GPX export free on Strava?

Exporting your own recorded activities is free. Creating custom routes from a GPX file requires a Strava subscription. Apps like Steps include free GPX export with no paywall.

### Are Strava alternatives as accurate?

For steps and distance, yes — HealthKit apps read from the same iPhone and Apple Watch sensors. Strava's strength is its social and segment features, not raw tracking accuracy.

### Will I lose my history if I switch?

No. Your workouts and steps sync to Apple Health, so any HealthKit app like Steps keeps your history. You can also export your data from Strava before leaving.
