# How Long Does It Take to Walk 12 km?

**It takes about 2 hours 24 minutes to walk 12 km at the average adult pace of 5 km/h, roughly 2 hours at a brisk 6 km/h, and closer to 3 hours at a slow 4 km/h.** Add 15 to 25 minutes of breaks and a realistic door-to-door total for 12 km is **2 hr 40 min to 3 hr**.

Twelve kilometres is 7.5 miles, about 15,600 steps, and a genuine half-day walk. That matters, because 12 km is past the distance where you can just multiply your one-kilometre time by twelve and call it done. Pace drift, water stops, and traffic lights all show up in the total. This guide gives you both numbers: the theoretical walking time and the one you will actually see on the clock.

## How Long to Walk 12 km at Each Pace

Speed is the biggest lever. Between a slow amble and a power walk there is more than two hours of difference over 12 km.

| Speed (km/h) | Speed (mph) | Effort level | Time to walk 12 km |
|--------------|-------------|--------------|--------------------|
| 3.0 km/h | 1.9 mph | Very slow / strolling | 4 hr 00 min |
| 3.5 km/h | 2.2 mph | Slow | 3 hr 26 min |
| 4.0 km/h | 2.5 mph | Casual | 3 hr 00 min |
| 4.5 km/h | 2.8 mph | Relaxed | 2 hr 40 min |
| 5.0 km/h | 3.1 mph | Average adult | 2 hr 24 min |
| 5.5 km/h | 3.4 mph | Purposeful | 2 hr 11 min |
| 6.0 km/h | 3.7 mph | Brisk | 2 hr 00 min |
| 6.5 km/h | 4.0 mph | Fast | 1 hr 51 min |
| 7.0 km/h | 4.3 mph | Power walk | 1 hr 43 min |

Most healthy adults land between 4.5 and 5.5 km/h on flat ground, which puts a 12 km walk at **2 hr 11 min to 2 hr 40 min** of moving time. Brisk walking — the moderate-intensity threshold the CDC uses in its physical activity guidance — brings 12 km in at almost exactly two hours.

If you know your pace in minutes per kilometre rather than km/h, the [pace to speed converter](/tools/pace-to-speed-converter) will translate it. As a shortcut: 12 km takes 12 times your per-km pace. A 12:00/km walker finishes in 2 hr 24 min.

## How Long Does It Take to Walk 12 km by Age?

Comfortable walking speed is remarkably stable from your 20s through your 50s, then declines. The figures below are averaged across men and women from Bohannon and Williams Andrews' meta-analysis of normal walking speed (*Physiotherapy*, 2011), which pooled comfortable gait speed across healthy adults by decade.

| Age group | Average speed | Speed (km/h) | Time to walk 12 km |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|--------------------|
| 20–29 | 1.35 m/s | 4.9 km/h | 2 hr 28 min |
| 30–39 | 1.39 m/s | 5.0 km/h | 2 hr 24 min |
| 40–49 | 1.41 m/s | 5.1 km/h | 2 hr 22 min |
| 50–59 | 1.37 m/s | 4.9 km/h | 2 hr 26 min |
| 60–69 | 1.29 m/s | 4.6 km/h | 2 hr 35 min |
| 70–79 | 1.20 m/s | 4.3 km/h | 2 hr 47 min |
| 80+ | 0.96 m/s | 3.5 km/h | 3 hr 28 min |

Two caveats worth knowing. First, gait-speed studies measure short courses of 3 to 30 metres, so they capture your fresh, unfatigued pace. Over 12 km almost everyone settles 5 to 10% below that, which adds roughly 10 to 15 minutes. Second, the spread inside each decade is wide — a trained 65-year-old routinely out-walks an untrained 30-year-old. For a fuller picture see [average walking pace](/blog/average-walking-pace) and [average mile walk time by age](/blog/average-mile-walk-time-by-age).

## The Realistic 12 km Total (With Breaks)

This is where 12 km separates itself from shorter distances. A 2 km walk is one continuous effort. A 12 km walk is a small logistical event, and three things eat into the clock.

**Pace drift.** Untrained walkers typically lose 5 to 10% of their starting speed in the final third. If you begin at 5.5 km/h, the last 4 km may come in nearer 5.0 km/h. That alone costs 6 to 10 minutes.

**Stops.** Water, snacks, a bathroom, resetting a sock, a photo. Expect two or three stops of 3 to 8 minutes each.

**Interruptions you did not plan.** Urban walks lose 1 to 3 minutes per kilometre to crossings and lights. A rural or trail route trades those for gates, mud, and gradient.

| Starting pace | Moving time | Realistic total (with breaks and drift) |
|---------------|-------------|------------------------------------------|
| 4.5 km/h | 2 hr 40 min | 3 hr 05 min – 3 hr 20 min |
| 5.0 km/h | 2 hr 24 min | 2 hr 45 min – 3 hr 00 min |
| 5.5 km/h | 2 hr 11 min | 2 hr 30 min – 2 hr 45 min |
| 6.0 km/h | 2 hr 00 min | 2 hr 15 min – 2 hr 30 min |

Block out **three hours** for a first 12 km attempt. If you finish in 2 hr 40 min, you have gained twenty minutes back. If you budget 2 hr 24 min and arrive at 3 hr, you are late for everything after it.

## 12 km in Miles and Steps

**12 km = 7.46 miles**, so call it seven and a half miles. That sits between a 10K and a half marathon, and it is close to the classic 8 km/5 mile weekend walk plus half again.

Step count depends on stride, which tracks closely with height:

| Height | Steps per km | Steps in 12 km |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| 5'0" (152 cm) | ~1,450 | ~17,400 |
| 5'4" (163 cm) | ~1,370 | ~16,400 |
| 5'8" (173 cm) | ~1,300 | ~15,600 |
| 6'0" (183 cm) | ~1,230 | ~14,800 |
| 6'4" (193 cm) | ~1,160 | ~13,900 |

The typical adult logs about **15,600 steps over 12 km**. ## Tips for Making 12 km Comfortable

- **Front-load the effort, not the pace.** Start 0.3 to 0.5 km/h below your target speed for the first 2 km. Negative splits feel far better at this distance than fading.
- **Eat before, not during, if you can.** Under three hours, a normal meal 60 to 90 minutes beforehand is usually enough. Carry a snack anyway.
- **Drink on a schedule.** Around 400 to 500 ml per hour in mild weather, more in heat. Do not wait until you feel thirsty at kilometre 9.
- **Shoes matter at 15,000 steps.** Half a size up, no cotton socks, and nothing brand new on the day.
- **Split the route mentally.** Three blocks of 4 km is easier to hold than one block of 12.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to walk 12 km at a normal pace?

At a normal adult pace of 5 km/h, walking 12 km takes **2 hours and 24 minutes** of moving time. With typical breaks and a small amount of pace drift, budget 2 hr 45 min to 3 hours from the moment you leave to the moment you get back.

### How long does it take to walk 12 km briskly?

At a brisk 6 km/h — the pace the CDC treats as moderate-intensity activity — 12 km takes exactly **2 hours**. Very fast walkers holding 7 km/h can finish in 1 hr 43 min, though sustaining that for the full distance takes training.

### Is walking 12 km a day too much?

For most healthy adults, 12 km a day is a large but achievable volume once you build up to it. It is roughly 15,600 steps and 2.5 to 3 hours of activity, well above the 150 weekly minutes of moderate exercise recommended by the CDC. Ramp up by no more than 10% per week and alternate long days with shorter recovery walks to avoid overuse injuries.

### How many miles is a 12 km walk?

12 km is **7.46 miles**, or about seven and a half miles. That is just over a 10K plus a 2K, and a little more than half a half-marathon distance.

### Can a beginner walk 12 km?

Yes, with a few weeks of preparation. If your current long walk is 5 km, add roughly 1 to 1.5 km to it each week and you will reach 12 km in five to six weeks. Expect a beginner time of 3 to 3.5 hours including breaks, at a comfortable 4 to 4.5 km/h. Working on [brisk walking](/blog/what-is-brisk-walking) technique will speed that up over time.
