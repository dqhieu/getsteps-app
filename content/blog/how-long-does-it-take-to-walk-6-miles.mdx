# How Long Does It Take to Walk 6 Miles?

If you're wondering **how long does it take to walk 6 miles**, the short answer is **about 2 hours** at a moderate 3.0 mph pace — or 1 hour 43 minutes at 3.5 mph, 1 hour 30 minutes at 4.0 mph, and 3 hours at a slow 2.0 mph. That's roughly 12,000 steps, or 9.66 kilometers, for most adults.

Your exact time still depends on speed, age, fitness, and terrain. Below are 6-mile times by pace and age, plus treadmill vs outdoor notes and how to split the walk into two sessions.

## How Long Does It Take to Walk 6 Miles by Speed?

Walking speed is the biggest factor in how long a 6-mile walk takes:

| Walking Speed | Pace (min/mile) | Time for 6 Miles |
|---------------|-----------------|------------------|
| 2.0 mph (slow) | 30:00 | 3 hours |
| 2.5 mph (casual) | 24:00 | 2 hours 24 min |
| 3.0 mph (moderate) | 20:00 | 2 hours |
| 3.5 mph (brisk) | 17:08 | 1 hour 43 min |
| 4.0 mph (power walk) | 15:00 | 1 hour 30 min |

Most adults walk between 2.5 and 3.5 mph. At a typical [average walking pace](/blog/average-walking-pace) of 3.0 mph, **how long does it take to walk 6 miles?** Exactly 120 minutes.

If you are unsure of your speed, use the [Walking Time Calculator](/tools/walking-time-calculator). A brisk 3.5 mph saves about 17 minutes versus the 2-hour baseline; a 4.0 mph power walk finishes in 90 minutes.

## Walking 6 Miles: Time by Age Group

Age affects walking speed more than many people expect. Research published in the *Journal of the American Medical Association* shows average walking speed declines gradually after age 50:

| Age Group | Avg Speed | Time for 6 Miles |
|-----------|-----------|------------------|
| 20–29 | 3.0–3.4 mph | 1 hr 46 min – 2 hr 0 min |
| 30–39 | 3.0–3.4 mph | 1 hr 46 min – 2 hr 0 min |
| 40–49 | 2.8–3.2 mph | 1 hr 53 min – 2 hr 9 min |
| 50–59 | 2.7–3.0 mph | 2 hr 0 min – 2 hr 13 min |
| 60–69 | 2.5–2.8 mph | 2 hr 9 min – 2 hr 24 min |
| 70+ | 2.1–2.5 mph | 2 hr 24 min – 2 hr 51 min |

These are averages, not a fitness test. A regular walker in their 60s can finish faster than a sedentary 30-year-old. Six miles is achievable at every age — the pace simply adjusts.

## How Many Steps Is 6 Miles?

Walking 6 miles is about **12,000 steps** for a person of average height, and typically lands between **12,000 and 13,500 steps** depending on stride. Taller walkers take fewer steps per mile; shorter walkers take more. Six miles is also **9.66 km**.

The average adult takes roughly 2,000 to 2,250 steps per mile. Use the [Steps Per Mile Calculator](/tools/steps-per-mile-calculator) for your personal count, then convert with the [Step Distance Calculator](/tools/step-distance-calculator). If you usually stick to [walking 2 miles a day](/blog/walking-2-miles-a-day), a 6-mile outing roughly triples that volume.

## How Long Does It Take to Walk 6 Miles on a Treadmill vs Outdoors?

Treadmill time matches outdoor time **at the same speed**. Set 3.0 mph and 6 miles still takes 2 hours. Set 4.0 mph and it still takes 1 hour 30 minutes.

A treadmill removes wind and uneven ground, so the same mph often feels slightly easier. A 1–2% incline mimics outdoor effort without changing the clock. Outdoors, trails, grass, or gravel can slow you 10–25% versus pavement, so a park loop may take closer to 2 hours 15 minutes even if your road pace is 3.0 mph.

## Splitting 6 Miles Into Two 3-Mile Walks

You do not have to walk 6 miles in one block. Two [3-mile walks](/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-walk-3-miles) — morning and evening — deliver the same distance, roughly the same 12,000 steps, and a similar calorie burn.

At 3.0 mph, each 3-mile walk takes about **60 minutes**. At 4.0 mph, each takes **45 minutes**. That split is easier to schedule than a single 2-hour outing and gives your feet a recovery window in between.

## Factors That Affect How Long It Takes to Walk 6 Miles

### Walking Pace and Fitness

Regular walkers can hold a brisk 3.5 mph for most of 6 miles. Beginners often settle near 2.5 mph, which stretches the outing to about 2 hours 24 minutes. Consistent walking usually improves speed within a few weeks.

### Terrain, Incline, and Weather

Flat pavement is fastest. Trails, grass, sand, or gravel slow you 10–25%. A moderate 5–10% grade can drop a 3.0 mph walker toward 2.5 mph. Heat, humidity, cold, and wind can cut sustainable speed another 10–15%. Bring water on any walk that lasts much more than an hour.

### Body Weight

Heavier walkers generally burn more calories per mile and may move a little slower. Over 6 miles the time difference is usually modest — a few minutes, not a different category of workout.

## How to Walk 6 Miles: Tips for Beginners

Six miles is a real commitment if you are new to walking for exercise. Most people can build up to it in 5–8 weeks. Start with 3- or [5-mile walks](/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-walk-5-miles) and add about half a mile each week. Five miles at 3.0 mph is 1 hour 40 minutes — a natural checkpoint before the full 2-hour outing. If a single session feels like too much, split it into two 3-mile walks.

Wear walking shoes with cushioning and a secure heel, and carry water on walks over an hour. A step counter app like [Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) shows distance, pace, and step count as you go.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to walk 6 miles at a normal pace?

At a normal walking pace of 3.0 mph, it takes **2 hours** to walk 6 miles. Most adults naturally walk between 2.5 and 3.5 mph, putting a typical range at 1 hour 43 minutes to 2 hours 24 minutes.

### How long does it take to walk 6 miles at 4 mph?

At 4.0 mph, 6 miles takes **1 hour 30 minutes**. That is a brisk power-walk pace — sustainable for fit walkers, but faster than a casual stroll.

### How many steps is a 6-mile walk?

A 6-mile walk is about **12,000 steps** for many adults, with a common range of 12,000 to 13,500 depending on height and stride. Six miles equals **9.66 km**.

### Can you split 6 miles into two walks?

Yes. Two 3-mile walks give you the same distance and roughly the same steps and calories as one 6-mile session. At 3.0 mph, each half takes about an hour.

### How many calories does walking 6 miles burn?

Walking 6 miles burns about **420 to 850 calories**, depending on body weight and speed. A 155-pound person typically burns around 490–540 calories at 3.0 mph.

### How long does it take to walk 6 miles on a treadmill?

The same as outdoors at the same speed: **2 hours at 3.0 mph** or **1 hour 30 minutes at 4.0 mph**. Add a 1–2% incline if you want the effort to feel closer to outdoor walking.

### Is walking 6 miles a day good for you?

Yes, for most healthy adults. A daily 6-mile walk is about 2 hours of moderate activity at 3.0 mph — well above the CDC's 150-minute weekly minimum in just two days. Build up gradually, or split the distance if a single session feels like too much.
