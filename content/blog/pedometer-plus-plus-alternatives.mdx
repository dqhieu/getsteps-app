# Best Pedometer++ Alternatives for iPhone (2026)

<TLDR>
The best **Pedometer++ alternative** for most iPhone users is **Steps: Workout & Pedometer** — it does everything Pedometer++ does, plus 23+ workout types, GPX export, 8 widget types, streak tracking, and a Wrapped-style yearly recap. For minimalist design, try StepsApp. For cross-platform (Android) and community challenges, try Pacer. All HealthKit apps count steps identically, so pick based on features, not accuracy.
</TLDR>

Pedometer++ is a beloved, long-running step counter for iPhone and Apple Watch. But maybe you want broader workout tracking, GPX export, richer widgets, or a fresh design — and that's exactly when people start hunting for **Pedometer++ alternatives**.

This guide covers the best Pedometer++ alternatives for iPhone in 2026, what each one is good at, and how to switch in seconds (no data migration needed).

## Why Look for a Pedometer++ Alternative?

Pedometer++ is excellent at what it does — counting steps and tracking walks, hikes, and outdoor adventures. But there are a few common reasons people look elsewhere:

- **You want more than walking** — multi-sport workout tracking for running, cycling, yoga, strength, swimming, and more
- **You want GPX export** — to save and share your routes
- **You want richer widgets** — more home screen, lock screen, and StandBy options
- **You want gamification** — daily streaks, personal records, and a yearly recap
- **You want a different look** — a fresh, modern design
- **You want Android too** — Pedometer++ is iOS and Apple Watch only

Whatever your reason, the good news is that switching is painless. Because every quality pedometer reads from Apple HealthKit, your step history follows you automatically.

## Best Pedometer++ Alternatives for iPhone in 2026

### 1. Steps: Workout & Pedometer (Best Overall)

[Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) is the most complete Pedometer++ alternative. It counts steps through HealthKit just like Pedometer++, then adds the features power users miss: 8 home/lock/StandBy widget types, a native Apple Watch app with complications, 23+ workout types with GPS, GPX export, daily goal streaks, 8 personal record categories, and a Wrapped-style yearly recap. Core features are free, with an optional Steps Pro upgrade.

**Best for:** Anyone who wants a pedometer *and* a full workout tracker in one app.

**Why it stands out:** Pedometer++ keeps things focused on steps, walks, and hikes. Steps gives you that plus everything else — making it the natural upgrade for people who've outgrown a step-only app.

### 2. StepsApp (Best Minimalist Design)

StepsApp is a clean, design-forward pedometer with a colorful interface, HealthKit sync, and a small set of widgets. If you love Pedometer++'s simplicity but want a more polished, modern look, StepsApp is a close match.

**Best for:** Users who want something simple and visually pleasing without a learning curve.

### 3. Pacer (Best for Cross-Platform & Community)

Pacer is one of the few Pedometer++ alternatives available on **both iPhone and Android**. It counts steps, offers guided walking programs, GPS route tracking, and group challenges with friends. If Pedometer++'s Apple-only nature is your blocker — or you want a social push — Pacer is worth a look.

**Best for:** Households split between iPhone and Android, or anyone motivated by community challenges.

### 4. Apple Health (Best Built-In, No Install)

Apple Health counts your steps automatically in the background — no app required. It's the data source every other app reads from. The catch: there are no home screen widgets, streaks, goals, or workout-logging tools of its own. It's a database, not a daily-use pedometer.

**Best for:** Minimalists who are fine opening the Health app manually and don't want to install anything.

### 5. Stepz (Best Free & Lightweight)

Stepz is a free, lightweight pedometer with a familiar progress-ring widget and basic charts. It's a no-frills option for people who just want a step count and a single glanceable widget.

**Best for:** Users who want a free, bare-bones step counter and nothing more.

## How to Switch from Pedometer++ to Steps

Switching is fast because your data already lives in Apple Health.

### Step 1: Install Steps

[Download Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) from the App Store and grant HealthKit access. Your full step history loads instantly — no manual export or import from Pedometer++ required.

### Step 2: Add a Widget

Long-press your home screen, tap **+**, search for Steps, and pick a widget. The circular progress ring shows steps, goal progress, and calories at a glance — a richer replacement for your old Pedometer++ widget.

### Step 3: Set Your Step Goal

Open Settings in the app and set your daily target. Not sure what to aim for? Our [daily step goal calculator](/tools/daily-step-goal-calculator) suggests a number based on your age and activity level.

### Step 4: Start a Streak

Hit your goal each day and your streak grows. Combined with 8 personal records and a yearly recap, it's the kind of motivation a step-only app can't offer.

## What to Look For in a Pedometer++ Alternative

Not every alternative fits every person. Use these criteria to choose:

### Feature Depth

Do you only need steps, or do you also track runs, rides, hikes, and gym sessions? If it's the latter, choose an app with multi-sport workout tracking like Steps.

### Widgets and Glanceability

The best pedometers let you check progress without opening the app. Look for variety across home screen, lock screen, and StandBy.

### Data Ownership

Your fitness data should be portable. GPX export lets you save and share routes — a feature most step-only apps skip.

### Motivation

Streaks, personal records, and yearly recaps turn step counting into a habit. If you need a nudge to stay consistent, prioritize these.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best alternative to Pedometer++?

For most iPhone users, Steps: Workout & Pedometer is the best Pedometer++ alternative. It matches Pedometer++ on step counting and adds 23+ workout types, GPX export, 8 widgets, streaks, personal records, and a yearly recap.

### Is there a Pedometer++ alternative for Android?

Yes. Pedometer++ is iOS and Apple Watch only, but Pacer works on both iPhone and Android. Google Fit and the Fitbit app are also cross-platform step-tracking options.

### Are Pedometer++ alternatives as accurate?

Yes. Every pedometer that uses Apple HealthKit reads from the same iPhone and Apple Watch motion sensors, so step counts are identical. Accuracy doesn't vary between apps — features do.

### Will I lose my step history if I switch?

No. Your step history is stored in Apple Health, not inside Pedometer++. Any HealthKit-based alternative like Steps loads your full history the moment you grant access. No migration needed.

### Are these Pedometer++ alternatives free?

Steps, StepsApp, Pacer, and Stepz are all free to download with optional paid upgrades. Apple Health is built into iOS at no cost. Core step tracking is free in every option.
