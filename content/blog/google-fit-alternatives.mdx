Google Fit is winding down. Google has announced that the Google Fit APIs will stop working at the end of 2026, with the app being replaced by Google Health and step data moving to Health Connect on Android. On iPhone, Google Fit was always a lightweight option — no real widgets, no native Apple Watch app — so its shutdown is a natural moment to switch to something built for iOS.

This guide covers the best **Google Fit alternatives** for iPhone in 2026 and how to move over in seconds.

## Why Look for a Google Fit Alternative?

Beyond the shutdown itself, here's why iPhone users are switching now:

- **Google Fit is being retired** — APIs end in 2026 and the app is being replaced
- **It was limited on iPhone** — no home screen widgets, no native complications
- **You want proper iPhone integration** — HealthKit sync, widgets, and Apple Watch support
- **You want free GPX export** — to save and share routes without a subscription
- **You want a yearly recap** — a Wrapped-style summary of your year in steps

The good news: on iPhone your steps are already stored in Apple Health, so switching to a HealthKit app is instant. (If you also used Google Fit on Android, export your historical data from Google before the shutdown.)

## Best Google Fit Alternatives for iPhone in 2026

### 1. Steps: Workout & Pedometer (Best Overall)

[Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) is the best Google Fit alternative for iPhone. Core features are free, with an optional Steps Pro upgrade.

**Best for:** iPhone users who want a properly integrated step and workout tracker after Google Fit.

**Why it stands out:** Google Fit never offered iPhone widgets or a native Watch app. Steps is built for iOS from the ground up, with glanceable widgets and a yearly recap in a generous free tier.

### 2. Pacer (Best for Cross-Platform)

If you're moving off Google Fit but still need an app that works on **both iPhone and Android** — for a mixed-phone household or a shared challenge — Pacer is the best fit. It offers step tracking, guided walking programs, and group challenges across platforms.

**Best for:** People who need cross-platform tracking after leaving Google's ecosystem.

### 3. Pedometer++ (Best Minimalist Option)

Pedometer++ is a long-running, trusted step counter for iPhone and Apple Watch with a clean, no-clutter interface. If you only used Google Fit to glance at your step count, Pedometer++ is the simple, focused replacement.

**Best for:** Users who want the simplest possible step counter.

### 4. Apple Health (Best Built-In, No Install)

Apple Health counts your steps automatically in the background — no app needed, and it's the iPhone equivalent of Google Fit's basic tracking. The trade-off: no widgets, streaks, or workout-logging tools of its own.

**Best for:** Minimalists fine with opening the Health app manually and installing nothing extra.

## How to Switch from Google Fit to Steps

Switching is fast because your iPhone step data already lives in Apple Health.

### Step 1: Install Steps

[Download Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) and grant HealthKit access. Your iPhone step history loads instantly. (Export any Android-only Google Fit history from your Google account first.)

### Step 2: Add a Widget

Long-press your home screen, tap **+**, search for Steps, and choose a widget. The circular progress ring shows steps, goal progress, and calories at a glance — something Google Fit never offered on iPhone.

### Step 3: Set Your Step Goal

Open Settings and set your daily target. Not sure what to aim for? Our [daily step goal calculator](/tools/daily-step-goal-calculator) suggests a number based on your age and activity level.

### Step 4: Track a Workout and Export It

Start any of 23+ workout types, then export the route as a GPX file — free, no subscription. It's a feature Google Fit never had.
