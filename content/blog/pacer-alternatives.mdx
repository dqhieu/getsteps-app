## Why Look for a Pacer Alternative?

Pacer is genuinely good at challenges and guided walks. Still, here's why people look elsewhere:

- **Ads in the free tier** — a fully ad-free experience requires Pacer Premium
- **Paywalled coaching and insights** — the AI coach and advanced analytics are Premium-only
- **You want free GPX export** — to save and share your routes without a subscription
- **You want deep native workouts** — multi-sport tracking for running, cycling, hiking, yoga, strength, and more, built in
- **You want better iPhone widgets** — richer home, lock, and StandBy widgets than Pacer offers
- **You want a yearly recap** — a Wrapped-style summary of your year in steps

The best part: switching costs you nothing. Every quality pedometer reads from Apple HealthKit, so your step history follows you automatically.

### 2. Pedometer++ (Best Minimalist Option)

Pedometer++ is a long-running, fan-favorite step counter for iPhone and Apple Watch. It focuses on steps, walks, and hikes with a clean, no-clutter interface. If Pacer feels like too much app, Pedometer++ is the calm, focused alternative.

Apple Health counts your steps automatically in the background — no app needed. It's the data source every other iPhone pedometer reads from. The trade-off: no home screen widgets, streaks, or workout-logging tools of its own.

**Best for:** Minimalists fine with opening the Health app manually and installing nothing extra.

## How to Switch from Pacer to Steps

Switching is fast because your data already lives in Apple Health.

### Step 1: Install Steps

[Download Steps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/steps-workout-pedometer/id6746096378) and grant HealthKit access. Your full step history loads instantly — no manual export or import from Pacer needed.

### Step 2: Add a Widget

Long-press your home screen, tap **+**, search for Steps, and choose a widget. The circular progress ring shows steps, goal progress, and calories at a glance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best alternative to Pacer?

For most iPhone users, Steps: Workout & Pedometer is the best Pacer alternative. It matches Pacer on step counting and streaks, then adds an ad-free free tier, free GPX export, 23+ native workout types, 8 widgets, personal records, and a yearly recap.

### Is Pacer free?

Pacer is free to download but shows ads, and features like the AI coach, virtual adventures, and advanced insights require Pacer Premium. Many people look for alternatives to avoid the ads or subscription.

### Are Pacer alternatives as accurate?

Yes. Every pedometer that uses Apple HealthKit reads from the same iPhone and Apple Watch motion sensors, so step counts are identical. Accuracy doesn't vary between apps — features and pricing do.

### Will I lose my step history if I switch?

No. Your step history is stored in Apple Health, not locked inside Pacer. Any HealthKit-based alternative like Steps loads your full history the moment you grant access. No migration needed.

### Is there a Pacer alternative for Android?

Pacer itself runs on Android, and so does StepsApp if you want a different cross-platform option. Steps and Pedometer++ are iOS and Apple Watch only.
